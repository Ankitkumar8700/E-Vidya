The Problem

In rural schools, students often fear English because it is completely alien to them. Outdated teaching methods lead to a lack of interest and massive drops in daily school attendance.

Our 'Crazy' Solution

Welcome to the Gamified Bilingual Learning Hub. We replace boring blackboards with a glowing interactive digital canvas. It teaches English using the native Hindi language.

More importantly, the classroom acts like a video game! Students unlock Attendance Rewards, earn points, and level up just by coming to school.
